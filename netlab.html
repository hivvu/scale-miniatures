Netcode lab

Two machines running the same race, both in this tab, with a wire between them you can make as bad as you like. Drive the left one: W accelerate, S brake, A/D steer. The right one is the other player's screen, which hears about your keys however late the slider says and guesses in the meantime.

The two hashes at the bottom cover all 64 KB of game state on each side. They must stay equal. Corrupt one breaks the right-hand machine on purpose, to show that a desync is actually noticed rather than hoped against.
