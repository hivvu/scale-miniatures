Loading the game

F8 does this from inside the game

This page is not serving the game. Point it at your own installed copy of Micro Machines and it will start. Your browser reads the folder locally: nothing is uploaded.

It has to be the installed directory, the one with MICRO.EXE and a GAME1 folder in it. A ZIP of floppy images is not enough on its own: run the original INSTALL.EXE once under DOSBox and keep the directory it produces.

The port

Not an
emulator

We disassembled the original MICRO.EXE and rewrote it, routine by routine, as TypeScript. What runs on this page is the game's own logic: its physics, its AI, its sprite renderer and its OPL2 sound sequencer, all expressed in another language.

Every round is in. The whole front end, Challenge and Head to Head, the music, the pause screen and the cheats. It is checked against thousands of frames and dozens of physics traces captured from the real game.

Also this one has something that the original could not do: Widescreen support.

Keyboard

The keys the game ships with. F5 on GAME OPTIONS changes them, and what you choose is remembered.

ActionPlayer 1Player 2
Steer AD ←→
Accelerate, brake WS ↑↓
Fire, confirm Alt Insert

The arrows and Return also drive player 1, right up until a two-player game starts, when they go back to being player 2's own keys. Esc goes back a screen, Space pauses a race, and a click skips the Codemasters intro.
