    Before you start

    Setting up

    Pick the tracks and the order to race them in, anything from 3 to 40 laps, and 2, 3 or 4 cars, with the seats nobody takes driven by the computer. There are points after every race and a table at the end.

    Driving

    W to accelerate, S to brake and reverse, A and D to steer, Alt to fire. The arrow keys work too.

    Your screen

    Everybody watches their own car, so what you see is not what they see. The helmets down the left are the running order, yours among them, and the number above them is the laps you have left.
