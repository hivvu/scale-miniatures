Micro Machines

Click the page first. Player one: A/D steer, W/S accelerate and brake, Alt fires and confirms. The arrows and Enter do the same until a two-player game starts, and then become player two's own keys (Left/Right/Up/Down and Insert). Esc goes back. A click skips the Codemasters intro, and ?nointro leaves it out altogether. Space pauses a race. The game starts on GAME OPTIONS, where F1-F4 set the controls, F5 redefines the driving keys, F7 calibrates a gamepad and Return plays; what you choose there is remembered. A gamepad counts as a joystick (steer with the stick, first button accelerates, second brakes) and the mouse needs a click to lock the pointer.

wider shows more track, and in Head to Head a bigger area before a car is left behind. F8 on the game's own GAME OPTIONS screen does the same thing without leaving the game, under the names in brackets. Double-click the picture for fullscreen too. Esc leaves fullscreen; press it again to go back a screen in the game.

This page is not serving the game. Point it at your own installed copy of Micro Machines and it will start. The folder is read in your browser and stays on your machine: nothing is uploaded.

It has to be the installed directory, the one with MICRO.EXE and a GAME1 folder in it. A ZIP of floppy images is not enough on its own: run the original INSTALL.EXE once under DOSBox and keep what it produces.
