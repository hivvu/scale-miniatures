Micro Machines

Click the page first. Player one: A/D steer, W/S accelerate and brake, Alt fires and confirms. The arrows and Enter do the same until a two-player game starts, and then become player two's own keys (Left/Right/Up/Down and Insert). Esc goes back. A click skips the Codemasters intro, and ?nointro leaves it out altogether. Space pauses a race. The game starts on GAME OPTIONS, where F1-F4 set the controls, F5 redefines the driving keys, F7 calibrates a gamepad and Return plays; what you choose there is remembered. A gamepad counts as a joystick (steer with the stick, first button accelerates, second brakes) and the mouse needs a click to lock the pointer.
